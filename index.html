About

A little about the person behind the pixels.

I’m Nidharnarani, a Digital Product Designer interested in understanding why people do what they do — and what happens when design takes that seriously.

I’m drawn to projects involving everyday problems, culture, emotions, technology and the strange little behaviours people don’t always notice.

My process usually starts with a question, gets slightly messy somewhere in the middle, and eventually becomes a screen, prototype, system — or occasionally something that doesn’t look like a screen at all.
